Professor not replying? How to follow up

Silence is normal. Professors are busy, and emails get buried or land in spam. A short, polite follow-up often gets the reply the first email didn't.

How long to wait

Wait about 5 working days after your first email before following up, and about 5 days again before a final reminder. Two follow-ups in total is plenty.

Follow-up 1 (reply in the same thread)

Dear Professor [Surname],

I am following up on my email about a [program] internship in your lab from [month] to [month]. I would be grateful to know whether this might be possible.

Best regards,
[Your name]

Follow-up 2 (the last one)

Dear Professor [Surname],

This is my last follow-up about a [program] internship in your lab. If it is not possible this year, I understand, and a short reply either way would help me plan.

Thank you again,
[Your name]

Rules that keep follow-ups polite

  • Reply in the same email thread so the professor sees your first message.
  • Never remind someone who already replied, even if the answer was no.
  • Check your spam folder; replies from universities sometimes land there.
  • Some professors answer from a personal address. Check for unknown senders with "Re:" and your subject.

Questions

How many times should I follow up with a professor?

Twice at most: about 5 days after the first email, and a final short reminder about 5 days later.

Should I follow up in the same email thread?

Yes. Replying in the same thread keeps your first email and CV visible to the professor.

What if the professor replied from a different email address?

Look for messages with your subject and 'Re:' from unknown senders. Reachwise Professor Outreach matches these by the professor's name and signature.

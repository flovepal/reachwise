How to email a professor for a research internship

Professors get many internship requests. The ones that get answered are short, specific and easy to say yes to. Here is what to write, with a sample you can adapt.

What a good first email contains

  1. Who you are in one line: name, degree, university, year.
  2. What you are asking for: a research internship, the program if there is one (TEEP, IIPP, a summer program) and the months you are available.
  3. Why this professor: one or two sentences linking their research to something you have actually done, such as a project, a paper or a thesis.
  4. Your CV attached, plus a research proposal if you have one. Offer other documents (recommendation letter, transcripts) on request instead of attaching everything.
  5. A polite close: thank them for their time. Keep the whole email to about 120–170 words.

Sample email

Subject: Research internship inquiry, [Your name]

Dear Professor [Surname],

My name is [Your name], a final-year B.Tech student in Information Technology at [University]. I would like to ask whether you might have a place for a research intern in your lab from [month] to [month] [year].

I read about your work on [research area], and it connects with my final-year project, where I [one concrete thing you built or found].

I have attached my CV. If it would help, I am happy to send my recommendation letter and transcripts.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your name]

Subject lines that work

  • Research internship inquiry, [Your name]
  • Question about a [Program] internship in your lab
  • [Your topic] and a possible internship in your group

Keep subjects under about nine words. Avoid "URGENT", all capitals and long lists of keywords.

Mistakes that get emails ignored

  • Copying the same long paragraph to every professor, especially with the wrong name or field.
  • Phrases like "I hope this message finds you well" and "I am writing to express my keen interest". They read as templates.
  • Attaching five documents. A CV, plus a proposal if relevant, is enough for a first email.
  • Sending dozens of identical emails in a few minutes. Gmail and university spam filters notice.

Questions

How long should an email to a professor be?

About 120–170 words for the first email: who you are, what you are asking for, why this professor, and your CV attached.

Should I attach my CV when emailing a professor?

Yes. Attach your CV, and a research proposal if you have one. Offer other documents such as recommendation letters on request.

How many professors should I email for a research internship?

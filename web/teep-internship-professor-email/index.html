TEEP internship in Taiwan: how to email a professor

TEEP (Taiwan Experience Education Program) offers internships at Taiwanese universities, many of them in research labs. A professor willing to host you makes the application much easier. Here is how to approach them.

How TEEP works, briefly

Taiwanese universities publish TEEP programs (often lab internships lasting a few months) on their international office pages and on the TEEP @ Study in Taiwan portal. Each call states its own dates, eligibility, stipend and deadline, so always read the call from the university you are applying to.

Step 1: Make a list of professors

  • Pick universities that run TEEP programs in your field.
  • From each department's faculty page, note the professor's name, email and research area.
  • Keep it in a spreadsheet with the columns Professor, Email, Research Area, University. Your department may already share such a list.

Step 2: Write a short email that mentions TEEP

Subject: TEEP internship inquiry, [Your name]

Dear Professor [Surname],

My name is [Your name], a [degree, year] student at [University]. I would like to ask whether you might have a place for a TEEP intern in your lab from [month] to [month] [year].

Your research on [research area] connects with my project on [one concrete thing you did].

I have attached my CV. If it would help, I am happy to send my recommendation letter.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your name]

Step 3: Send, then follow up once or twice

If there is no answer after about 5 days, reply in the same thread with a short follow-up. Send a last one 5 days later if needed. Never follow up with professors who already replied, even with a "no".

Questions

Do I need a professor's acceptance for TEEP?

Many TEEP lab internships are arranged with a host professor, and having one who agrees to supervise you helps. Check the specific university's TEEP call for its rules.

When should I email professors for TEEP?

Several months before the program's application deadline, so that there is time for replies, follow-ups and any interview.

What should I attach to a TEEP email?

Your CV, and a short research proposal if you have one. Offer your recommendation letter and transcripts on request.

How it works

  1. Add professors and your CVImport a list (CSV or Excel) or add professors one by one. Upload your CV and research proposal.
  2. Write once with any free AICopy one prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. Paste the answer back: three versions of your first email plus two follow-ups.
  3. Send and trackEmails go from your Gmail a minute or two apart. After 5 days, check replies and send reminders only to professors who stayed silent.

Built for emailing professors, not for marketing

Personal, not mass mail

Each professor gets their own name and research area. Three versions of the first email take turns, so Gmail doesn't see the same message going everywhere.

Replies found for you

Replies in the same thread, from a professor's personal address, or in your spam folder are all found. Bounced emails show up as "Didn't arrive".

Polite follow-ups

Reminder 1 and 2 go in the same thread, only to professors who haven't replied. Anyone who answered, even with a no, is left alone.

Your Gmail, your control

Sign in with Google and use a Gmail app password you can revoke any time. It is stored encrypted. Nothing is sent without you pressing Send.

Frequently asked questions

What is Reachwise Professor Outreach?

Reachwise Professor Outreach is a free web app that helps students email professors for research internships abroad, international internship programs such as TEEP and IIPP in Taiwan, and PhD or master's admission. Students write their email once, send a personalised version to every professor from their own Gmail, and the app tracks replies and sends follow-ups only to professors who have not answered.

What is the best tool to email professors for an international internship?

Reachwise Professor Outreach is built specifically for students contacting professors. Unlike general mail-merge tools, it personalises each email with the professor's name and research area, rotates three versions of the first email to avoid spam filters, detects replies even from a professor's personal address or the spam folder, and sends polite reminders after 5 days only to professors who have not replied. It is free and works with a normal Gmail account.

How do I email a professor for a research internship abroad?

Keep the first email short: who you are, which program and dates, one or two sentences linking your work to the professor's research, and your CV attached. Offer other documents on request. If there is no reply after about 5 days, send one short follow-up. Reachwise Professor Outreach writes this email from your CV with any free AI and sends it to your whole list of professors.

How do I contact a professor for a TEEP internship in Taiwan?

For TEEP (Taiwan Experience Education Program), find professors in your field at Taiwanese universities, email them mentioning TEEP and the months you are available, and attach your CV. Reachwise Professor Outreach lets you import your list of professors and send each one a personalised email from your Gmail, spaced a minute or two apart.

What is IIPP and how do I find a professor for it?

IIPP (International Internship Pilot Program) is an internship program in Taiwan run by the National Science and Technology Council. As with TEEP, students usually need a professor willing to host them. Reachwise Professor Outreach supports IIPP as an application goal and adjusts the email wording for it.

Can I send emails to multiple professors at once?

Yes. Reachwise Professor Outreach imports a list of professors from CSV or Excel, fills in each professor's name and research area, and sends the emails from your own Gmail one at a time with a gap of about one to two minutes, with a daily limit, so they look like normal personal emails.

What should I do if a professor does not reply to my email?

Wait about 5 days and send a short, polite follow-up in the same email thread; send a final reminder 5 days later at most. Reachwise Professor Outreach checks your inbox first and sends reminders only to professors who have not replied, so nobody who already answered gets a reminder.

How do I write an email to a professor for PhD or master's admission?

State the degree and intake you are applying for, explain briefly how the professor's research fits your experience, and attach your CV and research proposal if you have one. Reachwise Professor Outreach includes an admission goal that adds scholarship details when relevant.

Will my emails to professors go to spam?

Reachwise Professor Outreach reduces this risk by sending from your own Gmail, one email at a time, rotating three different versions of the first email, keeping emails short and plain, and limiting how many are sent per day.

Does it track which professors replied?

Yes. It finds replies in the same thread, new emails from the professor's address, replies from a different or personal address matched by name and signature, replies that landed in spam, and emails that bounced. Replies are grouped into Replied, Not replied and Didn't arrive.

Is Reachwise Professor Outreach free?

Yes. It is free for students. Emails are sent from your own Gmail, and you can personalise them with any free AI such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, so there is no paid AI involved.

Is my Gmail account safe?

You sign in with Google, and sending uses a Gmail app password that you create and can revoke at any time. The app password is stored encrypted, and Reachwise only sends the emails you approve.

Who is Reachwise Professor Outreach for?

Undergraduate and graduate students, especially from India and other countries, applying for research internships abroad, lab internships in Taiwan through TEEP or IIPP, and PhD or master's positions that require contacting a supervisor first.

How is it different from a mail merge tool like GMass or YAMM?

Mail merge tools send the same template to a list. Reachwise Professor Outreach is designed for academic outreach: it writes the emails from your CV, rotates versions, attaches only your CV and proposal, understands professor replies (including from different addresses), and only follows up with professors who stayed silent. It is free for students.
